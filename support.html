Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress, settings and hints are saved only on your device. There are no accounts and no cloud saves, so deleting the app or moving to a new device starts the game from the beginning.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings from the main menu and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy.

How does energy work?

In Hard and Challenge modes, a failed level costs one energy. Energy refills by one every 15 minutes, even while the game is closed. You can also watch an optional ad to get one energy right away.

How do I get more hints?

You get free hints every day in the shop, extra hints for beating some bosses, and you can watch an optional ad or buy a hint pack. Your hints are shared across all three modes.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are requested through them: on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com, and on Android through your Google Play order history.
