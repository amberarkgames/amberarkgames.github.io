Circuitified Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Türkçe: Gizlilik Politikası

Who we are

This privacy policy applies to Circuitified, a puzzle game for iOS and Android (the "app"), developed and published by Amber Ark Games (Gökberk Arıkan), an independent developer ("we", "us").

Contact for any privacy question or request: amberarkgames@gmail.com

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or any account. The app has no sign-in.
  • We do not run our own servers and we do not receive or store your personal data.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device data as described below.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your device so the game works: your level progress in each mode, the current board, energy and timers, your hint balance, your sound, music and vibration settings, and whether you have bought the ad-free upgrade.

This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob, a service of Google, to show ads. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process data such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped).

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the app's privacy settings.
  • On iOS, the app asks for permission before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

If you don't allow personalized ads, you may still see non-personalized ads. Google processes this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Google may process data outside your country.

In-app purchases

Purchases (such as hint packs and the ad-free upgrade) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. They handle your payment information under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google). The app only receives a confirmation of the purchase so it can unlock what you bought. We never receive your card or payment details.

Notifications

The app may ask for permission to send you a notification when your energy is full again. These notifications are scheduled on your device. No data is sent to us. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Diagnostics

We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own. If you have agreed to share diagnostics with app developers on your device, Apple or Google may give us anonymous, aggregated crash and performance reports. These reports do not identify you.

Children

Circuitified is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us and we will help.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete on our side. Game data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted according to Google's policies. You can reset your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.

Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage on your device. The advertising and purchase services used by the app communicate over encrypted connections.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the KVKK in Türkiye), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we don't store personal data about you, most requests about advertising data are best directed to Google. You are always welcome to contact us at amberarkgames@gmail.com and we will help.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics or ads. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gets new features. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top.
