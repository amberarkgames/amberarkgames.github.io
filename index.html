Circuitified

Rotate. Connect. Power the core.

A neon circuit puzzle where every wire matters. Turn the tiles until the power flows through every node and the whole grid closes into a glowing loop.

Coming soon to the App Store & Google Play

The game

Each level is a scrambled power grid. Tap a tile to rotate it, guide the current out of the reactor core and close every circuit. No loose ends, no dead wires.

Three modesRelaxed Normal mode, Hard mode with move limits and Challenge mode with time and move limits.
Boss levelsBigger grids every ten levels, plus Ultra and Uber bosses in Hard and Challenge.
Special circuitsCorroded conduits, crossover bridges, quantum-linked twins, unstable cores, stealth holo-nodes and induction coils.
Built from codeNeon visuals, sound effects and music are all generated for the game.

Good to know

Circuitified is free to play. It shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases, such as hint packs and an ad-free upgrade. You don't need an account, and your progress is saved on your device.
